About Prestige ✷

Do you miss the beginning of OTIS when you could go up ten levels from submitting just one unit? Do you wish $\sqrt x$ was a little less convex?

The Prestige ritual lets you sacrifice {{ max_level }} levels to obtain a special Prestige star indicator next to your level in its place. (This terminology is adapted from video games.) After activating Prestige, you can then continue to level up again. There is no limit to the number of Prestige stars you can obtain.

Activating Prestige in this way does not revoke access to the Ruby Palace or previously obtained secret units.

How Prestige is calculated

You get to choose how to allocate the 100 levels offered. For each level, a permanent deduction is applied to reduce the total to the targeted perfect square.

For example, if you have 1000♣ (which is 31 Dexterity) and you choose to offer 15 Dexterity (for 16 Dexterity remaining). Then afterwards you will have 256♣.
