About Prestige ✷

Do you miss the beginning of OTIS when you could go up ten levels from submitting just one unit? Do you wish $\sqrt x$ was a little less convex?

The Prestige function lets you exchange {{ max_level }} levels to obtain a special Prestige star indicator next to your level in its place. (This terminology is adapted from video games.) Activating Prestige in this way does not revoke access to the Ruby Palace or previously obtained secret units.

After activating Prestige, you can continue to level up. There is no limit to the number of Prestige stars you can obtain.

Note this action is irreversible!
