A unit petition lets you request changes to your units. Here you can open new petitions and see the status of previous ones.

  • Your first few petitions are auto-approved by OTIS-WEB.
  • After that, petitions will be manually reviewed by Evan. This may take 1-2 days, but usually not longer than that.
  • If you submit an egregious amount of petitions relative to the number of completed units (i.e., you are requesting units with no intention of actually working on them), then OTIS-WEB may rate-limit you and prevent further petition usage. In that case, you'll need to contact Evan manually and beg for forgiveness.

Instructions

There are currently four permitted actions.

  • Unlock: The unit is added if it wasn't there already, and moreover the unit is automatically unlocked. The first few petitions of this type are automatically processed; the rest require manual review from Evan.
  • Add for later: The unit is added to the list, but not unlocked. Petitions of this form are processed automatically.
  • Lock: The unit is removed from the unlocked list, but not deleted from your list. Note this does not automatically "replace" the dropped unit.
  • Drop: This removes a unit entirely from your list. (Be careful what you wish for!) Note this does not automatically "replace" the dropped unit.
