Submission requirements

Thank you for your interest in submitting suggestions! We're happy to hear your ideas.

At the same time, we should warn you that OTIS quality standards are high! For a suggestion to be considered, you will have to adhere to house style and expect to put quite a bit of work into solution writing.

General instructions:

  • You MUST follow all the requirements in BOTH of the following two documents:

    Production quality (PDF) LaTeX style guide (HTML)

    If you don't, your submission will simply be returned with revisions requested. (OTIS-WEB can catch some of the LaTeX violations automatically, but not all.)

    In particular, you must write your own solution; see also the AI/LLM policy.

  • If you are suggesting a problem from a fairly well-known source (e.g. IMO Shortlist), there is a decent chance Evan might have done the problem already; double-check first.

  • Legal: By submitting, you are granting Evan permission to use, edit, or adapt your entire submission, either for OTIS or any other math-olympiad-related purposes.

    On the other hand, you retain copyright/ownership of your writing, so you can also use it for whatever you want elsewhere.

  • We strongly recommend that you type up your LaTeX in an external document and copy-paste it here when you're done. This web form is not intended to be a web editor.
