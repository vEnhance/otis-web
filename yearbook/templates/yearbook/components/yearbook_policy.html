⚠️ Before you sign the yearbook

The yearbook is entirely optional, but it has a couple of rules you should know about first.

  • The yearbook uses real names. Your page will be titled with the first and last name on your OTIS account (currently {{ request.user.get_full_name|default:request.user.username }}), not your username or a nickname. If that isn't your real name, please email Evan to have it corrected before you sign.
  • Your years in OTIS are shown. Every semester you were enrolled as an OTIS student is listed on your page automatically.
  • Everyone in the Verified group can read it. That's all current and past OTIS students and staff — several hundred people. It is not public to the whole internet, but it is not private either, so don't put anything here you wouldn't want that whole group to see.
  • Everything else is up to you. Every other field is optional, you can edit them whenever you like, and you can delete your entry to leave the yearbook at any time.
