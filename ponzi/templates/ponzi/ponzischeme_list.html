Rules

Each Ponzi scheme minigame opens on a specified date and runs until its global pool of spades is exhausted.

  1. The global pool is initially empty. However, the current amount in the global pool is not shown throughout the game.
  2. In the game, you may bid anywhere from 1 to {{ max_bid }} spades, inclusive (integers only). You must wait 24 hours between bids and you cannot bid more spades than you have. Bids are added to the global pool for that scheme.
  3. Each game specifies a length of time $T$ one must wait before a bid reaches the next tier. During this time period, a bid cannot be withdrawn.
  4. Once it reaches a tier, you may withdraw it for that tier's interest rate, or re-invest it again to reach the next tier (after another $T$ time).
  5. The interest return rate for each tier is:
      {% for name, rate in tier_returns %}
    • Tier {{ name }}: {{ rate|floatformat:"-1" }}% return rate
      • {% endfor %}
  6. All withdrawals are paid out of the global pool.
  7. The scheme ends immediately once someone attempts to make a transaction which would make the global pool negative. In that case, the final transaction is cancelled and the scheme collapses. Any spades still bid are lost after the scheme collapses.
