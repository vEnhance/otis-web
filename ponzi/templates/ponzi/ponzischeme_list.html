Rules

Each Ponzi scheme minigame opens on a specified date and runs until its global pool of spades is exhausted.

  1. Up to once per day, you may bid anywhere from 1 to {{ max_bid }} spades, inclusive (integers only). This is added to the global pool for that scheme.
  2. A bid reaches the next tier two weeks after being placed. While growing, a bid can't be withdrawn.
  3. Once it reaches a tier, you may withdraw it with interest, or lock it up for two more weeks to reach the next tier.
  4. The return rate for each tier is:
      {% for name, rate in tier_returns %}
    • Tier {{ name }}: {{ rate|floatformat:"-1" }}% return rate
      • {% endfor %}
  5. All withdrawals are paid out of the global pool.
  6. The scheme ends immediately once someone attempts to make a transaction which would make the global pool negative. In that case, the final transaction is cancelled and the scheme collapses. Any spades still bid are lost after the scheme collapses.
  7. The amount in the global pool is hidden from players.
